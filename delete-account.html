Delete My Account

This page is for people who use the Ourworldhive apps (My Diary, E-Permit, PM Tracker, ZeroHarm, Project Planner and others) and want their account deleted.

📔 My Diary (Android app and website)

Developer: DUKEKRRISH (Ourworldhive) · App: My Diary – Tasks & Notes

  1. Open My Diary and sign in.
  2. Tap ⋯ (top right).
  3. Under Account, tap Delete My Account.
  4. Type DELETE and confirm.

Deleted: your sign-in account, every diary page, note, task, handwriting, reminder, repeating job and template, and your full-version record. Nothing of your diary is kept in the cloud. Export a backup first (⋯ › Backup) if you want to keep your pages.

Kept: a record of a purchase may be kept by Google Play and our payment log for tax and refund rules (up to 8 years). It holds no diary content.

To delete only some data without deleting the account, delete those pages or tasks inside the app; they are removed from the cloud too.

Other Ourworldhive apps

Delete inside the app

Open the app, go to More (in Project Planner: Account) and tap Delete my account. Type DELETE to confirm. If the app asks you to sign in again first, do that and delete right away.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account.
  • Your name and membership in the plants, companies or projects you joined.
  • Your saved links to those plants, companies or projects.

What stays

Records that your team needs, such as permits raised, safety observations logged and tasks marked done, stay with your plant or company as work records. They show the name that was entered at that time. If you want these records changed or removed too, send a request using the email below.

Request by email

If you cannot open the app, email er.krushnananda@gmail.com from the address you signed up with. Write "Delete my account", the app name and the plant or company code. We reply within 30 days.
