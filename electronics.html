Electronics Lab

Build a circuit, press Run and watch it work. Voltages, currents, an oscilloscope and a trend recorder, all inside your browser. No sign-up, nothing to install.

ready

Tap a part on the left to put it on the board.
Tap a round pin, then another pin, to join them with a wire.

Oscilloscope

Put Probe A or Probe B on any point of the circuit. Time per division is set at the top.

Trend recorder

Watch how voltage, current or power in any part changes over time, then download the numbers.

Every part you can use, with what it does and its key formula. Press \u201CTry it live\u201D to open a working circuit with the part in it.

Ready-made circuits. Each one opens on the board and starts running at once.

The lab is for learning and for quick checks of ideas. It is a simplified simulator, so real parts, fast signals and large circuits can behave differently. Never use it as the only check for anything that affects safety.
