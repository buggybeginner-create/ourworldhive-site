Delete My Data

You can delete some or all of your data without deleting your account. Everything you delete inside the app is removed from our cloud too, on every device.

📔 My Diary (Android app and website)

Developer: DUKEKRRISH (Ourworldhive) · App: My Diary – Tasks & Notes

Quickest way: open My Diary › ⋯ › Account › 🧹 Delete My Data. There you can:

  • Delete pages between dates: choose From and To dates (or This Page, Last 7 Days, This Month, Older Than 1 Year, All Dates) and tap Delete These Pages.
  • Delete all diary data: every page, task, handwriting, reminder, repeating job and template. Type DELETE to confirm. Your account and full version stay.

You can also delete single items:

To deleteDo this
A task or note lineTap the task, then tap 🗑️ Delete.
A whole day's pageDelete every task and line on that day and clear its handwriting. An empty page is removed from the cloud automatically.
HandwritingOpen the pen, then tap 🗑️ Clear handwriting, or select strokes and delete them.
A reminder or alarmTap the task › ⏰ Remind › Remove.
A person or phone number linked to a taskTap the name on the task › Edit › Remove name.
A template📋 Templates › 🗑️ Delete.

To delete all your data and the account, see Delete My Account.
