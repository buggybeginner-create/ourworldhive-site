Number TrailStage 1

Level 1 · Easy⏱ 0:00
Drag from 1, then 2, 3, … and fill every square.
How to playStart at 1 and draw one path by dragging (or arrow keys). Visit the numbers in order 1 → 2 → 3 … and cover every square exactly once. The last number must be the last square you fill. Thick dark lines are walls: the path cannot cross them. Drag back over your path to undo, or press Undo. A hint costs a star (and 10 seconds in challenges).
